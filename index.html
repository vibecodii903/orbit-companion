Start with least privilege. Add capabilities one at a time, only when there is a clear use case.

Permission levels
Level 0 · Observe
Read what you explicitly enter into this app.
Level 1 · Suggest
Offer plans and draft actions; no external execution.
Level 2 · Ask first
Future: request approval before sending, changing or deleting anything.
Level 3+ · Limited automation
Not implemented. Must use narrow scopes, logs and a kill switch.
Important: Do not give an AI unrestricted access to your phone, passwords, bank account, email or shell. Approval should be specific to each sensitive action, and revocable.
